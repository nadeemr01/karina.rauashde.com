About Karina.

Care for skin, hair and nails — personal to every patient.

I’m a dermatologist with more than five years of experience as a doctor, now practicing at Ashab Clinics in Riyadh.

I diagnose and treat acute and chronic conditions of the skin, hair and nails — acne, rosacea, psoriasis, eczema, seborrheic dermatitis and more. I perform minor surgical procedures such as biopsies and excisions, and offer a full range of laser, injectable and aesthetic treatments.

Every treatment plan is built around the patient’s skin type and skin tone. I studied General Medicine at Volgograd State Medical University and completed my dermatology residency in Moscow — at RUDN University and the State Research Center of Dermatovenereology and Cosmetology. I also trained in trichology at RUDN and in cosmetology at the First Professional Institute of Aesthetics.

In November 2026 the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties (SCFHS) classified me as Registrar in Dermatology. Arabic is my native language, and I also work in Russian and English.
