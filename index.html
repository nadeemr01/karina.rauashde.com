About

Skin, hair and nails — treated with precision and care

I am a dermatologist with more than five years of experience as a doctor, focused on disorders of the skin, hair and nails. I diagnose and treat acute and chronic skin conditions — acne, rosacea, psoriasis, eczema and more — perform minor surgical procedures such as biopsies and excisions, and offer a full range of laser, injectable and aesthetic treatments.

Every treatment plan is built around the patient’s skin type and skin tone. I studied General Medicine at Volgograd State Medical University, completed my dermatology residency and trichology training at RUDN University, and trained in cosmetology at the First Professional Institute of Aesthetics in Moscow.

Arabic is my native language, and I also work in Russian and English. In November 2026 I passed the Saudi Medical Licensure Examination (SMLE).
